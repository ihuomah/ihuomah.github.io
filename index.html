Background

About Kyma

I came to cybersecurity from the ground up — building a foundation in network engineering and Linux systems administration through years of hands-on independent practice, then five years of service with the Nigerian Army, first as a Computer Science Instructor and Head of Department within the Army Education Corps, then as a Simulation Officer modelling how complex systems behave under operational stress.

That progression brought me to Heriot-Watt University, where my MSc dissertation investigates whether large language models can generate reliable CVSS scores — comparing structured prompting (PDL) against conversational prompting, measured against official NVD scores and EPSS data.

I also run CreativeKyma — a cybersecurity education platform translating complex security concepts for non-technical audiences including organisations, small businesses, and children. Because security only works when people understand it.
